Check before you crimp. Fields marked verify are the ones to confirm against the controlling spec or the manufacturer's catalog before they reach a drawing. Field structure is stable; individual code meanings drift between revisions and between vendors.

Distributor pricing and lead times come from Mouser and DigiKey and are indicative only — they reflect catalog stock, not a quote against your quantity, contract, or flight-hardware traceability requirements.

Decoding runs in the page. Distributor queries go through this site's own server so no API key is exposed.
